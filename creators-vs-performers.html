The Anomaly of the Creator

In their paradigm, my vision and my scale are viewed as a pathology of a grandiose ego, a set of illusions and dreams, a complete detachment from reality. Yet, my intellect is simply a given, and my intuition is not an attempt to assert superiority. My scale is evaluated through the lens of their paradigm as narcissism and escapism, simply because I do not belong to their reality; I cannot force myself into their framework.
Consequently, the system they are part of reads me as someone who has fled from reality, when in truth, I simply belong to a different reality; I belong to another world. And when I am forced into their space, there is naturally no place for me among them. To them, it looks as though I possess grandiose delusions. The very system they live in reads me as a foreign, anomalous object that cannot be integrated anywhere.
I have zero interest in being a performer. Because I am a creator—I create. And if I create, then for them, and for the system they occupy, such a thing cannot exist; the option of a 'creator' simply does not compute. In their eyes, if you consider yourself a creator, you are a narcissist whose imagination and illusions have run wild. The position of a creator does not exist in their coordinate system; that option is entirely absent.
That is the ultimate catch. They read me as an object that cannot be fitted into any slot within their system, whereas I simply belong to a completely different system. My system is smaller compared to theirs, because 99% of the people around me belong to the matrix of performers. As a crushing majority, they oppress me simply because I am entirely alone at this given moment.
There are others like me, but they are scattered across the globe; I just need to find them. When you are alone in this framework, it is undeniably brutal to withstand such a massive crowd and their system. To them, these words are just grandiose illusions. You cannot prove anything to them. They do not understand; for them, such a concept does not even exist. How am I supposed to exist among them? The only way is to leave them entirely.
