the Creator

In their paradigm, my vision is viewed as a pathology of a grandiose ego, a set of illusions and dreams, a complete detachment from reality. My scale is evaluated as narcissism and escapism. The system they are part of reads me as someone who has fled from reality, whose imagination has run wild; it defines me as a foreign, anomalous object that cannot be integrated anywhere simply because I do not belong to their reality; there is no place for me among them. The position of a creator does not exist in their coordinate system. The option of a creator does not compute as it is entirely absent.
